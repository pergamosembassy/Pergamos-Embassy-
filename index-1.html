What is Pergamos?

Pergamos Embassy is a Christ-centered ministry for young people, campuses and hungry believers. We teach the Word deeply, pray, disciple, and send people out as representatives of Christ.

The name comes from the church in Pergamos in Revelation 2: a people who stay faithful to Christ in a world of competing voices.

You were not called only to attend Christ. You were called to become like Him.

Based on 2 Corinthians 3:18.

Our creed

We are a people being transformed into Christlikeness.

We choose presence over performance. We pursue truth without pride. We value Scripture and seek the presence of God.

We believe gifting must grow alongside character. We pursue purity because we belong to Christ.

We pray because we know we cannot build without God. We fellowship because transformation was never designed to happen in isolation.

We serve because the Kingdom is something we build, not merely something we attend. We lead through faithfulness, character and fruit.

We raise generations rather than followers. We honor one another, embrace correction, pursue restoration, and carry one another.

Above everything else, we desire to become like Jesus.

Leadership is stewardship

Presence earns observation. Faithfulness earns responsibility. Character earns trust. Fruit earns leadership.

Who we are becoming

We do not make a stereotype. Someone shaped by Pergamos increasingly shows love for Christ, biblical understanding, humility, purity, prayer, discipline, teachability, service, courage, compassion and discernment. They can say: I am still becoming.

A Pergamos leader is

Spiritually alive, biblically grounded, character-driven, faithful, teachable, humble, relational, responsible, pure, servant-hearted and reproducible.

Depth produces humility

Revelation is a responsibility, not a badge. The more someone grows, the more approachable they become.

Honest conversation, correction and restoration

You can bring real questions here: relationships, sexuality, doubts, academics, family, purpose, identity and failure. A question is not rebellion. We talk about purity honestly, without shame, because we pursue holiness out of love for Christ.

Correction

Biblical, respectful and restorative. We correct because we care about who you are becoming.

Restoration

Failure does not define a person. Repentance, accountability and healing lead back to a future.

Safety and boundaries

Because we serve young people, we keep clear standards around mentorship, privacy and safeguarding. No leader uses prophecy or position to control anyone's personal decisions.

Honor

Dignity for parents, leaders, teachers and one another, while staying truthful.
